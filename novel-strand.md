Novel Strand is a useful example of the kind of place SOSO can highlight beyond outdoor recreation: a neighborhood spot where the reason to go is partly the people and programming.

It has operated as both a daytime cafe and brewery and has hosted recurring events such as trivia, book clubs and other community gatherings. Check its current calendar before going.
