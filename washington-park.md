Wash Park is one of the easiest places in Denver to just go be outside. It has two lakes, broad paths, lawns, gardens and a steady stream of runners, cyclists and neighborhood groups.

It also has an active stewardship community. Denver Parks & Recreation and Friends and Neighbors of Washington Park have organized recurring cleanup and planting projects here.
