Herman Gulch is a classic Front Range hike with a quick transition from forest into big alpine views.

### SOSO field note

When I visited, we crossed paths with a mushroom-foraging group on the trail. It turned into one of those unexpected experiences that makes you start noticing an entirely different layer of the landscape.

### Before you go

High-country weather can change quickly. Check current trail, road and weather conditions before heading out.
