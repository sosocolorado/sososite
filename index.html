Field Note No. 001

Herman Gulch

Clear Creek County, Colorado

Activity Hiking
Distance About 6.5 miles
Elevation gain About 1,700 ft
Season Summer – early fall
Setting Forest + alpine

Why go

Herman Gulch is an accessible introduction to Colorado's high country, beginning just off I-70 and climbing from forest into increasingly open alpine terrain.

The trail eventually reaches Herman Lake beneath the surrounding peaks. Wildflowers, changing vegetation and expansive views make the journey as worthwhile as the destination.

When I visited, we crossed paths with a mushroom-foraging group on the trail. It turned into one of those unexpected experiences that makes you start noticing an entirely different layer of the landscape.

Before you go: Conditions in Colorado's high country can change quickly. Check current weather, trail and road conditions before heading out.

About SOSO

A reason to look closer.

I made SOSO because I love exploring new places and getting to know the place I live.

It's part personal field journal and part resource for anyone looking for somewhere new to hike, wander, camp, ski, soak, gather or get involved.

The goal isn't to check every place off a list. It's to make it easier to discover what's around us — and build a stronger relationship with the landscapes and communities we call home.
