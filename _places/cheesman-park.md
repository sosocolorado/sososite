Cheesman is the kind of park that works without much planning: bring a blanket, walk a loop, read under a tree, or meet friends near the pavilion.

Denver identifies Cheesman as one of the major parks in the East Central area. The park includes paved and crusher-fines trails, large lawns and the historic pavilion.
